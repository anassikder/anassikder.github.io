# Rahbar privacy policy

**Effective date:** 10 November 2026

Rahbar (রাহবার · رهبر) is an Islamic lifestyle app for Android. It works offline. This
policy explains what Rahbar keeps, where it keeps it, and the few ways information can leave your phone,
all of which you start yourself. It applies to Rahbar in every one of its languages.

Rahbar comes in two editions. **The APK** you install yourself goes online for one thing only: downloading the
audio, when you ask for it. **The Google Play
edition** is the same app with one banner ad, Rahbar Pro (which removes the ad) and audio you download after
installing; it goes online for those three things only. What the Play edition adds is explained in
[The Google Play edition](#the-google-play-edition) below; everything else in this policy applies to both.

## In short

- The APK connects to the internet **only when you tap Download** to get the audio, and it sends nothing you enter
  in Rahbar, to us or to anyone else. See [Audio downloads in the APK](#audio-downloads-in-the-apk).
- The Google Play edition connects only to show its ad (through Google AdMob), to sell Rahbar Pro (through
  Google Play) and to download the audio (from Google Play). It sends nothing you enter in Rahbar to anyone.
- There are **no accounts, no analytics and no tracking by us**. Ads appear only in the Play edition, and not
  at all with Rahbar Pro.
- Everything you enter stays **on your phone**. We, the developers, never receive it.
- You can delete all of it at any time with **Erase all data**, by clearing Rahbar's storage, or by
  uninstalling the app.

## What Rahbar keeps on your phone

| Information | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Your name, photo, date of birth and gender (all optional) | To greet you, show your age and Hijri birthday, and personalise the profile | App storage on your phone |
| Your location: the place your prayer times are for (coordinates, the nearest city's name) | To work out prayer times, the Qibla direction and nearby mosques | App storage on your phone |
| Your home place, if you turn on travel mode | To say how far you are from home when you travel | App storage on your phone |
| Your mosque, if you add one: its name, where it is (when picked from the mosque list), its jamaat times and your jamaat reminder | To show the jamaat times and remind you before each | App storage on your phone |
| Your prayer log, Quran reading position, bookmarks, memorization, tasbih counts, lessons, quiz results and learning points | To show your progress and pick up where you left off | App database on your phone |
| Your fasting log (the days you fasted or missed, and whether a fast was for Ramadan, to make one up, or voluntary) | To show your Ramadan and the fasts you have kept | App database on your phone |
| Your qada counts (the prayers and fasts you owe, and how many you have made up) | To help you keep track of making them up | App database on your phone |
| The daily deeds you tick, and any deeds you add yourself | To show your day, your week and your streak | App database on your phone |
| Your review cards: the key words you have collected from finished lessons, how well you knew each one and when it comes back | To bring each word back for review before you forget it | App database on your phone |
| Your stage exam and placement test results, the stage the placement test started you at, and the school of fiqh you chose in a lesson | To show which stages you have passed and their certificates, to start you at the right stage, and to pick out your school's view | App database and app settings on your phone |
| Your settings, including the zakat and Zakat al-Fitr figures you type | So the app remembers your choices and your last calculation | App storage on your phone |
| Links to audio files or folders you choose (a custom adhan, extra reciters) | To play them | App storage on your phone; Rahbar only reads these files |

The Quran, hadith, duas, translations and recordings that come with the app are content, not information
about you.

Rahbar does not collect your contacts, messages, call logs, device identifiers or advertising ID. (In the Play
edition, Google's ad SDK reads the advertising ID to show ads; see [The Google Play edition](#the-google-play-edition).)

## Permissions and what they are used for

| Permission | Used for |
|---|---|
| Location (approximate and precise) | Finding where you are, once, when you ask Rahbar to use your location. You can choose a city from the list instead. If you turn on travel mode, also once when you open Rahbar (see below). Never in the background. The location is used on the phone only. |
| Notifications | Prayer alerts and the reminders you turn on, the jamaat reminder among them. |
| Alarms and reminders (exact alarms) | Showing prayer alerts on time. |
| Run at startup | Setting your reminders again after the phone restarts. |
| Vibration, keep awake, foreground service (media playback) | Playing the adhan and Quran recitation, including with the screen off. |
| Internet and network state | Downloading the audio when you tap **Download**. The network state tells Wi-Fi from mobile data, so Rahbar can ask before using your data. In the Google Play edition, also the ad and Rahbar Pro. |

The Google Play edition also has these, for the three things described in
[The Google Play edition](#the-google-play-edition):

| Permission | Used for |
|---|---|
| Google Play billing | Buying Rahbar Pro, and asking Google Play whether you have it. |
| Advertising ID, and Android's ad services (ad ID, ad attribution, ad topics) | Declared by Google's ad SDK, which may use them to show and measure ads. Rahbar itself doesn't read them. |
| Foreground service (data sync) | Keeping an audio download going from Google Play, with a notification, while you use other apps. |

Photos you pick go through Android's photo picker, and photos you take go through your camera app.
Rahbar gets only the picture you choose and keeps a small square copy for your profile.

### Travel mode

Travel mode is off until you turn it on (Profile › Settings › Prayer). When it is on:

- Each time you open Rahbar, it asks your phone for one location fix, at most once every half hour and never in
  the background. Without the location permission, or with location turned off, it does nothing.
- It compares that fix with your saved place on the phone. If you have moved more than 30 km, Home offers the
  prayer times for where you are.
- Rahbar keeps only a place you accept. If you tap "Not now", the fix is forgotten. If you chose "Change the
  place without asking", the new place is saved straight away, as if you had accepted it.
- When you turn travel mode on, your current place is kept as your home place, so Rahbar can tell you how far
  you are from home.

### The home-screen widget

If you add Rahbar's widget to your home screen, it reads your saved place and calculation method on the phone
to show the next prayer and today's times. Like any widget, it can be seen by anyone who sees your home screen.
It needs no permission and sends nothing anywhere.

## When information can leave your phone

Only in these cases, and only when you start them:

1. **Android's device backup.** If you have turned on backup for your phone, Android includes Rahbar's
   data (profile, progress, fasting log, qada counts, deeds, bookmarks and settings, your mosque and places
   among them) in your phone's backup to your Google account and in phone-to-phone transfers, as it does for
   other apps. Google handles this under your Google account's terms; Rahbar does not see or control it. You
   can turn it off in your phone's settings.
2. **Backup files you export.** Settings › Backup & restore saves a file with your data (including your fasting
   log, qada counts and deeds) and your profile photo to a place you choose. Anyone you give that file to can
   read it.
3. **PDFs you save.** The progress report and the zakat statement are saved where you choose and contain
   what they show.
4. **Sharing.** When you share a verse, hadith, dua or article, the text goes to the app you pick. "Share
   Rahbar" in About sends a short message with a link to Rahbar's Google Play page.
5. **Maps.** Opening directions to a mosque passes the mosque's location to your maps app. Outside Bangladesh,
   "Search in a maps app" on the mosque page passes your saved place and the word "mosque" to it. Your maps
   app may use the internet under its own privacy policy.
6. **Reading aloud.** When you ask Rahbar to read a hadith, or a Bangla translation of the Quran that
   has no audio of its own in the app, aloud, the text is passed to your phone's text-to-speech engine,
   which works under its own privacy policy. Muhiuddin Khan's translation plays from audio inside the app
   and passes nothing on.
7. **Sending feedback.** "Send feedback" in About opens **your own email app** with our address filled in and a
   subject line naming Rahbar's version and language. Rahbar sends nothing itself: nothing leaves your phone
   unless you write and send that email, and you can change or delete anything in it first. If you send it, we
   receive what you wrote and your email address, through your email provider, and use them only to answer you.
8. **Rating.** "Rate Rahbar on Google Play" opens Google Play (or your browser, if Play isn't there) at
   Rahbar's page. Those apps work under their own privacy policies; Rahbar itself stays offline.

## Audio downloads in the APK

The Quran recitations, the Bangla translation audio and the Forty Hadith readings don't come with the APK. When you tap
**Download** on Profile › Audio downloads, your phone's own download manager fetches them from **GitHub**, a service run
by GitHub, Inc., which sees your internet address as any website does and works under its own privacy policy. Rahbar sends
nothing else: not your name, location, logs or anything you enter. We don't receive any record of the download. The audio
then stays on your phone and plays without the internet. Nothing is downloaded until you ask, and the app doesn't go
online for anything else.

## The Google Play edition

The edition installed from Google Play adds three things, each run by Google rather than by us:

- **One banner ad**, shown under the main tabs (never on a page of the Quran, a hadith, a dua or a lesson), from
  **Google AdMob**. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects, under Google's privacy policy:
  your phone's advertising ID and app set ID, its IP address (from which an approximate location can be worked
  out), information about the ads you see and tap, and diagnostic information about the ad SDK. In the European
  Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Rahbar first shows Google's consent form, and no ad is requested until
  you have answered; you can change your answer at any time in Profile › Rahbar Pro › Ad privacy choices.
  Elsewhere you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's
  settings (Google › Ads, or Privacy › Ads). Ads are limited to those rated suitable for everyone. Rahbar does
  not give AdMob anything you enter in the app: not your location, your profile, your logs or your progress.
- **Rahbar Pro**, a subscription sold through **Google Play** that removes the ad and changes nothing else. Google
  Play handles the payment under its own terms; we never see your card or payment details. Rahbar asks Google
  Play only whether you have Pro, so it knows whether to show the ad.
- **Audio downloads**: the Quran recitations, the Bangla translation audio and the Forty Hadith readings are
  downloaded from **Google Play** when you ask for them, and played from your phone afterwards.

With Rahbar Pro, no ad is requested and the consent form isn't shown.

## Languages

Rahbar comes in 12 languages. Choosing one changes nothing about your information: the translations, the
books in each language are installed with the app, and Rahbar downloads nothing when you switch. A language's books are copied inside Rahbar's own storage the first time you read
in it, and stay there with the rest of the app's content.

## Erasing your data

**Erase all data** (Profile › Offline content) deletes everything Rahbar keeps on this phone: your profile and
photo, prayer log, fasting log, qada counts, deeds, bookmarks, progress, your mosque, your places and your
settings. It also cancels Rahbar's reminders. The app then starts again as if newly installed. It cannot be
undone, and it does not touch:

- a backup file you exported (delete that yourself if you want it gone);
- a copy in your Google account's backup, which Android replaces the next time it backs up Rahbar, and which you
  can remove from your Google account's backup settings.

Uninstalling Rahbar, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes the same information.

## How long information is kept

Until you delete it. You can remove individual items in the app (a bookmark, a deed you added, a day's mark),
restore or replace data from a backup, or remove everything with Erase all data. Because nothing is stored on
any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; the only exception is an email you choose to send
us, which you can ask us to delete.

## Children

Rahbar itself collects nothing from anyone, children included (the Play edition's ad is served by Google AdMob,
as described above). It is not directed at children under 13: some of the
books it carries, such as the six hadith collections, discuss adult subjects (marriage, divorce, legal
punishments) as their sources do.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what Rahbar does with information, this policy will be updated before
that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
